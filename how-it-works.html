// 8-STEP METHODOLOGY

The Complete Production Workflow

Every stage enforces a dual-approval gate: FAMEWOVEN studio execution paired with client team sign-off before proceeding.

01 STAGE // IMMERSION

Kickoff & Brand Immersion

Sets up joint Slack channel, shared Notion workspace, competitor visual audit, and target buyer persona mapping. Aligns on campaign KPIs and budget parameters in a single 30-min call.

02 STAGE // IDENTITY

Character Architecture & Persona Bible

Designs fictional adult character geometry, apparent age, styling rules, voice tone, and reference sheet (01 AI Influencer Creation). You review and approve the character concept before any model training.

03 STAGE // TRAINING

Dataset Curation & LoRA Model Locking

Generates 15 to 50 multi-angle studio lighting studies (front, 3/4, profile, macro) and trains dedicated character LoRA weights. Verifies 360-degree facial consistency across expressions and lighting conditions.

04 STAGE // DISTRIBUTION

Campaign Brief & Creator Shortlisting

Storyboards single-action product moments and audits human amplification creators across 141 engagement parameters. Your team selects preferred creators from the shortlist.

05 STAGE // STUDIO

Still & Motion Studio Production

Renders 4K editorial stills, product interaction shots, and 5s–30s vertical 9:16 video drafts with motion capture and lip-sync. Reviews take place inside our staging portal.

06 STAGE // COMPLIANCE

Human Safety, Claims & Disclosure Audit

Performs human retouching, verifies product depiction accuracy, and embeds AI-GENERATED CHARACTER and FTC disclosures. Grants final commercial and legal sign-off.

07 STAGE // WAVE

Synchronized Launch Wave (T−7 to T+3)

Coordinates T−7 creator seeding, T−0 launch wave posts, T+1 quote reposts, and T+3 deep-dive threads in a 48h window. Monitors live rollout velocity via real-time telemetry.

08 STAGE // ATTRIBUTION

Attribution Report & Continuity Archive

Compiles plain-language reach, impression, CTR, and conversion reports; archives character weights for the next release (04 Ongoing Content). Locks the roadmap for next month.
