// FAQ

Questions, answered.

Everything you need to know about custom AI characters, rights ownership, escrow, and launch timelines.

01 // How do you maintain the exact same virtual character face across outfits and videos? +

Every character is built on our Identity Layer: we generate a 15 to 50-angle studio reference dataset, train a dedicated custom LoRA model, and apply strict prompt anchoring with human post-production direction so facial geometry, age, and natural skin texture remain 100% consistent across stills and 9:16 video.

02 // Do we own the commercial rights to a custom AI brand character? +

Yes. When you commission a bespoke character under Pillar 01 (AI Influencer Creation), full commercial usage rights and optional category exclusivity transfer to your brand. Alternatively, you can license personas from our 100+ Virtual Talent Roster on a campaign or monthly basis.

03 // How do you label virtual talent and handle platform compliance? +

In accordance with our 12/VOICE charter, we label virtual talent clearly and describe what we make. Every deliverable includes compliant AI-GENERATED CHARACTER / CONCEPT metadata, platform-native synthetic media labels (Meta, TikTok, X), and FTC endorsement disclosures.

04 // How do you choose human creators for amplification campaigns? +

We start from who you sell to, never from vanity follower counts. We audit each creator across 141 parameters—including audience demographics, real comment engagement, niche authority (AI, SaaS, fashion, beauty, dev tools), and past sponsored conversion—before you review and approve the shortlist.

05 // What is the minimum budget and how does zero-risk escrow work? +

A single sponsored creator post or stock avatar shoot starts under $2,500, whereas bespoke character systems and 50 to 100+ creator launch waves range from $10,000 to $50,000+. For creator sourcing, your payment is held in zero-risk escrow and released only once posts are verified live.

06 // How fast can a character or campaign launch? +

Single posts and existing roster shoots can go live within 3 to 5 business days of an approved brief. Custom AI character development and coordinated multi-creator launches typically require 7 to 14 days so every asset is reviewed and timed to your launch window.

07 // Can we approve every image, video, and creator post before it goes live? +

Yes. You review and sign off on character turnarounds, wardrobe styling, video drafts, creator scripts, and posting schedules inside our staging workflow before a single post is published.

08 // Do you offer ongoing monthly retainers? +

Yes. Through Pillar 04 (Ongoing Content) and our Long-Term Creator Partnerships, we run monthly releases so your brand character and creator advocates maintain a recognisable, compounding presence on the timeline.
